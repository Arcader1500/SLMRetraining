Decode: penalize only what the model generates

A repetition penalty on generated tokens, not the note, stops loops without hurting extraction. Tested on 10 cases with an early checkpoint.

Greedy (1.00)Penalty 1.08Penalty 1.15

At 1.08, valid JSON rose from 5 to 9 of 10 and 2,048-token loops fell from 4 to 0. At 1.15 the penalty started to break structure.
